# The YACM name and logo

"YACM" and the YACM logo identify the original YACM project. The GNU
GPL lets everyone use, change and share the code; it does not cover the name
and the logo (NOTICE.md, term e). This policy says how they may be used.

## Allowed without asking

- Talking about YACM: articles, reviews, tutorials, talks, comparisons.
- Saying that your work is based on YACM, as term (b) of NOTICE.md requires.
- Sharing unmodified official releases under the name YACM.
- Packaging YACM for a package manager or an operating system (winget,
  Homebrew, Flathub, Linux distributions and similar) under the name YACM,
  with changes needed only for packaging that do not change what YACM does.
  Say in the package description that it is packaged by you.

## Needs written permission

- Using the name or the logo for a modified version, a fork or another
  product, or a name that could be confused with YACM.
- Using the logo on merchandise, in advertising, or in a way that suggests
  your product, service or company is made, supported or endorsed by the
  YACM project or its authors.
- Domain names, app store entries or social media accounts with the name YACM.

Ask at support@yacm.app.
